Summary

Mixed methods UX researcher across enterprise SaaS and developer tools. I run the quantitative side myself: SQL over usage data, survey design, benchmarking instruments, NLP on open text. I pair the quantitative what with the qualitative why, then connect the result to adoption and revenue. I have stood up research functions from scratch: built a solo practice into a four-person team, shipped the first research repository and intake workflow, and published templates that made insights self-serve for partners who were not researchers.

Experience

AuditBoard (SaaS, GRC)

Staff UX Researcher (first embedded UX researcher; grew the practice solo into a four-person team)

  • Built research operations from zero: intake and prioritization workflow, first research repository as the department’s single source of truth, and a standardized benchmarking program that cut study cycles from eight weeks to four.
  • Ran 30+ interviews with customers, non-customers, and director-level stakeholders; nine jobs-to-be-done and three vision stories became the product organization’s top three priorities and anchored a board-level plan to 3x revenue in five years.
  • Set the quality bar directors enforced on every new product launch: an 80/100 usability threshold.
  • Designed scenario-based hiring protocols that screened for reasoning over polish; both hires succeeded immediately. Interim manager of the four-person team for six months.

GitLab (developer tools, B2B SaaS)

Senior UX Researcher, Security

  • Built six security personas from 20+ interviews, each mapped to opportunity areas, design principles, and benchmarkable tasks; template published to the research repository for repeat use across squads.
  • Outcome-Driven Innovation survey uncovered 25+ application security opportunities, prioritized across eight market segments.
  • Two annual documentation surveys, 1,200+ responses analyzed, paired with qualitative follow-up; shipped templates so squads answered recurring questions without waiting on a researcher.

Higher Logic (community SaaS)

Senior UX Researcher / UX Researcher

  • Built the Engagement Benchmarking Score, an analytical instrument from expert interviews and statistical modeling mapping behavioral engagement segments to success metrics; later integrated into the product’s dashboard.
  • Led market segmentation and benchmarking research; findings shaped the 2021 Engagement Benchmarking Report and contributed to over $1 million ARR in the first year.
  • Applied NLP (topic modeling, sentiment) to 1,200+ open-response survey entries, turning free text into trackable themes.
  • Revamped search UX from SQL usage-data analysis and rapid iterative testing: 15-point SUS increase within a fixed research budget.

Methods

Mixed methods is how I work, not a bullet point: interviews and jobs-to-be-done for the felt experience; surveys, A/B testing, and benchmarking for the behavioral claim. SQL, R, Python. NLP on open text: topic modeling, TF-IDF, sentiment analysis. Reads data-science output and chooses the method by the decision at stake.
