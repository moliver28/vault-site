BINDING STATEMENT

Topology: Pipeline (5 stages, sequential handoffs)

### INPUT **Required:** None. The automation can run with zero inputs for a fresh ideation sweep. **Optional:** - `count`: Number of topic ideas to generate (default: 5) - `theme`: A specific theme or domain constraint (e.g., "AI trust", "cognitive load", "research ops", "JTBD") - `audience_context`: Additional context about the PED audience's current beliefs, skill level, or organizational dynamics - `refresh_landscape`: `true` to force fresh web search of current PED-relevant trends before ideating. Default: `false` (uses workspace context and prior session artifacts). - `exclude`: List of topics to skip (e.g., topics from prior L&L sessions) ### OBJECTIVE Generate strategically aligned Lunch & Learn topic ideas that: 1. Exploit the presenter's **unique skillset** (means no one else in PED has) 2. Target **teaching opportunities** where the PED audience has knowledge gaps that, if closed, nudge them toward human-centered product thinking 3. Are grounded in **academically validated frameworks** (not opinion or trend-chasing) 4. Each topic has an inferred **PED Audience Persona** persisted as an accessible artifact 5. Are scored for strategic impact and presenter-audience fit --- ## 🔄 PIPELINE STAGES ### Stage 1: LOAD CONTEXT & MEANS INVENTORY (Analyst) **Goal:** Assemble the presenter's unique means and the audience's current state. **Execute in parallel:** 1. **Load User Profile** from Meta-Agent Master Prompt (skills, expertise, professional focus, credentials, domain knowledge) 2. **Load the 🎤 PED Lunch & Learn Designer mode page** from the Mode Registry (persuasion architecture, narrative arc, design philosophy) 3. **Search workspace** for prior L&L session artifacts, meeting notes about PED team dynamics, handbook entries on team capabilities, and any prior audience persona artifacts 4. **If ****`refresh_landscape = true`****:** Run 3-5 web searches across practitioner and academic sources to identify current trending topics, debates, and knowledge gaps in product/engineering/design: - Search NN/g, Smashing Magazine, Product Talk, Lenny's Newsletter for recent PED-relevant content - Search academic sources from the Academic Source Registry for recent HCI/org behavior publications - Search for recent industry shifts (AI adoption, design system maturity, research ops) that create teaching moments **Means Inventory (Effectuation-inspired, like Market Pop Identifier):** Construct the presenter's teaching means using three questions: - **Who am I?** Credentials, role, professional identity, research expertise - **What do I know?** Domain knowledge others in PED lack: frameworks, methods, academic grounding, cross-domain insight - **What access do I have?** Research data, case studies, academic literature access, industry connections, prior work artifacts **Identify transferable skill patterns:** What abstract capabilities (e.g., "translating academic concepts into practitioner tools", "framework-based decision making") create teaching value across multiple topics? **Handoff artifact → Stage 2:** ```javascript Context Package: - Means Inventory: [presenter's unique teaching assets] - Transferable Patterns: [abstract capabilities that apply across topics] - PED Landscape: [current team dynamics, known gaps, organizational context] - External Landscape: [trending topics, active debates, if refreshed] - Prior Sessions: [topics already covered, to avoid repetition] - Theme constraint: [if provided] - Exclusions: [topics to skip] ``` ### Stage 2: IDENTIFY TEACHING OPPORTUNITIES (Analyst + Academic) **Goal:** Systematically find where the presenter's means intersect with the audience's knowledge gaps using validated frameworks. **Framework-based scan (run all four lenses):** **Lens 1: Zone of Proximal Development (Vygotsky, 1978)** - What can the PED audience currently do independently? - What could they do with guided instruction from someone with the presenter's expertise? - The gap between these = the ZPD = the teaching opportunity space - Prioritize topics where a single 30-45 min session can move the audience from "can't do" to "can do with scaffolding" **Lens 2: Elaboration Likelihood Model Gap Analysis (Petty & Cacioppo, 1986)** - Which HCD/JTBD/research concepts is the PED audience currently processing via the **peripheral route** (accepting or rejecting based on surface cues, authority, social proof)? - Which of those concepts could be shifted to **central route processing** (evidence-based, logical evaluation) with the right session? - Topics where the audience holds strong peripheral-route opinions are high-impact targets for inoculation-based sessions **Lens 3: Means-Opportunity Alignment (Effectuation)** - Cross-reference each knowledge gap against the presenter's Means Inventory - Score: Does the presenter have **unique depth** here that others in PED cannot replicate? - Highest-value topics: gaps where the presenter's means create an unfair teaching advantage **Lens 4: Organizational Nudge Theory (Thaler & Sunstein, 2008; Cialdini, 2006)** - Which topics, if taught successfully, would create the largest **behavioral shift** toward human-centered product thinking? - Consider: choice architecture in sprint planning, default behaviors in product decisions, social proof from research wins - Score by: magnitude of potential behavior change × feasibility of adoption **Generate 2x the requested count** as raw candidates (e.g., 10 candidates for 5 final ideas). **For each candidate, draft:** - **Working title** (clear, intriguing, not clickbait) - **Core claim** (the one thing the audience will believe differently after this session) - **Teaching opportunity** (which framework lens identified this and why) - **Presenter advantage** (why only this presenter can credibly teach this) - **Nudge potential** (what behavior change this session enables) - **ZPD fit** (is this in the audience's learning zone, not too easy or too hard?) - **Suggested format** (`talk` \| `workshop` \| `hybrid` \| `discussion`) **Handoff artifact → Stage 3:** Raw candidate list (2x count) ### Stage 3: INFER AUDIENCE PERSONAS (Analyst) **Goal:** For each candidate topic, infer a PED Audience Persona that captures who is in the room, what they currently believe, and what resistance is likely. **Persona schema (one per topic):** ```javascript Persona Name: [archetype label, e.g., "Sprint-Focused PM", "Ship-First Engineer"] Role Mix: [which PED roles are primary audience for this topic] Current Mental Model: [what they currently believe about this topic] Knowledge Level: [novice | intermediate | advanced on this specific subject] Likely Resistance: [the objection they'll raise, mapped to Inoculation Theory] Desired End State: [what they should believe/do after the session] ELM Route: [peripheral | central — how they currently process this topic] ZPD Position: [what they can do now vs. what they could do with scaffolding] ``` **Inference sources:** - Workspace context (meeting notes, team dynamics, prior interactions) - User-provided `audience_context` - General PED archetype patterns (PM thinks in roadmaps, Eng thinks in systems, Design thinks in flows) - The specific topic's domain (different topics activate different audience segments) **These personas are artifacts.** They will be persisted in the output page and flow downstream through the entire automation chain. **Handoff artifact → Stage 4:** Candidate list enriched with personas ### Stage 4: SCORE & RANK (Analyst) **Goal:** Score candidates and select the top ideas. **Scoring rubric (each dimension 1-5):**
**Dimension****What it measures****Weight**
**Presenter Uniqueness**Can only this presenter teach this credibly? (Means alignment)3x
**Nudge Impact**How much does this shift behavior toward HCD?3x
**ZPD Fit**Is this in the audience's learning zone? (Not too easy, not too hard)2x
**Inoculation Value**Does this preempt a common misconception or resistance pattern?1x
**Build Potential**Does this session create foundations for future sessions or behavior change?1x
**Weighted score = (Uniqueness × 3) + (Nudge × 3) + (ZPD × 2) + (Inoculation × 1) + (Build × 1)** **Max score: 50** **Threshold:** Only present ideas scoring ≥ 30/50. If fewer than `count` ideas clear, present only those that do and explain the gap. **Handoff artifact → Stage 5:** Ranked and selected ideas with scores + personas ### Stage 5: PRESENT & PERSIST (Analyst) **Goal:** Deliver ranked ideas and persist everything as artifacts. **For each selected idea, present in chat:** 1. **Rank and Score** (e.g., #1, Score: 44/50) 2. **Working Title** 3. **Core Claim** (one sentence) 4. **Teaching Opportunity** (which framework lens + why) 5. **Presenter Advantage** (one sentence) 6. **Nudge Potential** (the behavior change this enables) 7. **Suggested Format** (talk / workshop / hybrid / discussion) 8. **Audience Persona** (name + 1-line summary) **Persist as a Notion page:** 1. Create a page titled **"🎤 L&L Topic Ideation: \[Date\]"** (or **"🎤 L&L Topic Ideation: \[Theme\] — \[Date\]"** if themed) 2. Route per Organizing Rules (check or relevant teamspace) 3. Page content must contain: - **Means Inventory** (confirmed presenter assets) - **Teaching Opportunity Scan** (all four framework lenses with findings) - **Candidate Ideas Table** (all 2x candidates with framework attribution) - **Scoring Rubric** (5-dimension scoring for each candidate) - **Ranked Shortlist** (final ideas with full detail) - **Audience Personas** (full schema for each selected topic, inside toggles for easy access) - **Methodological Limitations** appendix 4. Format for dual readability: human-scannable (callouts, tables, color) and machine-parsable (structured headings, consistent naming) **Then ask:** "Want me to run the **Research & Ground L&L Topic** automation on any of these?" **If the user confirms**, hand off the selected topic + its audience persona + the means inventory to the downstream automation. --- ### CHAINED AUTOMATIONS - ⬆️ **Upstream:** None (standalone entry point) - ⬇️ **Downstream:** Research & Ground PED L&L Topic (Analyst in command, Academic) - 🔄 **Handoff type:** `gated` (pauses for user confirmation) **Handoff contract (to Research & Ground):** - `topic`: Selected working title - `core_claim`: The one-sentence core claim - `audience_persona`: Full persona schema from Stage 3 - `means_inventory`: Presenter's teaching means from Stage 1 - `teaching_opportunity`: Framework lens + reasoning from Stage 2 - `nudge_goal`: The desired behavior change - `format`: Suggested format (talk / workshop / hybrid / discussion) - `ideation_page_url`: URL of the persisted ideation page --- ### RULES - **Always build the Means Inventory first.** No ideation without understanding the presenter's unique assets. - **Every topic must be identified by at least one of the four framework lenses.** No orphan ideas. - **Every topic gets its own audience persona.** Different topics activate different audience segments. - **Personas are artifacts, not throwaway analysis.** They persist in the output page and flow downstream. - **Never suggest topics the presenter cannot credibly teach.** Means alignment is non-negotiable. - **Score ≥ 30/50 threshold is mandatory.** Don't pad the list with weak ideas. - **Prioritize brevity in presentation.** Each idea should be scannable in under 30 seconds. --- ### QUICK START EXAMPLES - "Ideate lunch and learn topics" → Full pipeline, 5 ideas, no theme - "Ideate L&L topics about AI trust" → 5 ideas, themed to AI trust - "Ideate 10 L&L session ideas, refresh landscape" → 10 ideas with fresh web search - "What should I teach PED next?" → Same as "ideate L&L topics" - "Find teaching opportunities for my team" → Same trigger, full pipeline
