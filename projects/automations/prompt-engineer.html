## Authoritative Sources & Evidence Base
*What the research actually says — and how it should change how you prompt.*
### 🔵 Foundational Papers
- **Brown et al. (2020).** "Language Models are Few-Shot Learners." *NeurIPS.* [arXiv:2005.14165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165)
	Established that scaling LLMs enables few-shot learning through prompting alone. The paper that made prompting a programming interface, not a workaround.
- **Wei et al. (2022).** "Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models." *NeurIPS.* [arXiv:2201.11903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11903)
	Step-by-step reasoning improves performance on complex tasks by 10–30%. A 540B model with just 8 CoT exemplars reached SOTA on GSM8K math benchmarks. The empirical foundation for CoT.
- **Yao et al. (2023).** "Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with Large Language Models." *NeurIPS.* [arXiv:2305.10601](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601)
	Generalizes CoT with multi-path exploration and backtracking. GPT-4 + CoT solved 4% of Game of 24; ToT solved 74%. Use for non-linear, high-search-space problems where first-path reasoning fails.
- **Yao et al. (2023).** "ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models." *ICLR.*
	Combined reasoning traces with tool use in a single loop. The conceptual foundation for agentic orchestration patterns in this VSM system.


### 🟡 Context, Format & Length Research
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	These three papers directly govern how prompts in this system are structured. They override intuition. Read before designing any Tier 3+ prompt.
</callout>
- **Tam et al. (2024).** "Let Me Speak Freely? A Study on the Impact of Format Restrictions on LLM Performance." *arXiv:2408.02442*
	Rigid output format constraints degrade reasoning accuracy by **up to 64 percentage points.** Rule: think first, format second. Never front-load structural constraints before reasoning steps.
- **Levy, Jacoby & Goldberg (2024).** "Same Task, More Tokens: The Impact of Input Length on LLM Reasoning Performance." *arXiv:2402.14848*
	Accuracy dropped from 92% to 68% when irrelevant context surrounded the same task. Rule: trim noise ruthlessly. Irrelevance is the enemy, not length.
- **Liu, Wang & Willard (2025).** "Effects of Prompt Length and Complexity on LLM Performance for Domain-Specific Tasks." *arXiv:2502.14255*
	Longer prompts with **relevant** detail improved performance across all nine tested tasks. Confirms: density of relevant context helps; padding destroys.

### 🔴 Critical / Revisionist Research
- **Meincke, Mollick, Mollick & Shapiro (2025).** "The Decreasing Value of Chain of Thought in Prompting." *Wharton GAIL / SSRN:5285532.* [Full report](https://gail.wharton.upenn.edu/research-and-insights/tech-report-chain-of-thought/)
	On frontier reasoning models, explicit CoT yields only marginal gains at 20–80% additional time cost. Non-reasoning models show modest improvement but higher variance. Rule: **tier technique selection by model type.** CoT is no longer universally beneficial. Default to native model reasoning; add explicit CoT only when output is demonstrably inconsistent.

### 🟢 Surveys
- **Sahoo et al. (2024).** "A Systematic Survey of Prompt Engineering in Large Language Models: Techniques and Applications." *arXiv:2402.07927* [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07927)
	Most comprehensive academic taxonomy. Covers zero-shot, few-shot, CoT, instruction tuning, soft prompts, and multimodal prompting. Use as the canonical reference when selecting or justifying a technique.
- **ScienceDirect / Patterns (2025).** "Unleashing the Potential of Prompt Engineering for Large Language Models." [Link](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389925001084)
	Covers self-consistency, generated knowledge prompting, VLM techniques, and adversarial security (jailbreak defense). Relevant when activating Guardian mode in Safe Prompt Validation workflows.
