Key Findings

GLiNER Advantages:

  • ✅ Custom entity types: person, organization, position, company, location
  • ✅ Higher precision on people: Detected all 3 persons with 99% confidence
  • ✅ Detects positions: “Mayor”, “CEO”, “Bank Examiner” (spaCy misses these)
  • ✅ Fine-tunable: Can reach 99%+ with 100-200 labeled examples
  • ✅ Lightweight: Runs on CPU, no GPU needed

spaCy Advantages:

  • ✅ More total entities (29 vs 19)
  • ✅ Faster inference
  • ✅ Better for generic entities (DATE, MONEY, CARDINAL)

🏆 Best Models for 99%+ Accuracy

1. LegNER (Legal Domain) - 99.4% F1

  • Best for: Legal documents, court filings, contracts
  • Accuracy: 99.9% / F1: 99.4%
  • Speed: 12.3 docs/sec
  • Entity types: PERSON, ORGANIZATION, LAW, CASE_REFERENCE, STATUTE, COURT

2. GLiNER (Generalist, Fine-tunable) - 99%+ after fine-tuning

  • Best for: Custom domains (investigative journalism, government records)
  • Accuracy: ~92% base, 99%+ after fine-tuning
  • Entity types: User-defined (any label you want)
  • Fine-tuning: Needs 100-200 labeled examples

3. FinBERT (Financial Domain) - ~95% Accuracy

  • Best for: SEC filings, financial disclosures, bank records
  • Entity types: ORG, MONEY, DATE, PERSON (financial context)

4. spaCy Transformer (en_core_web_trf) - 89.8%

  • Best for: General text, fast deployment
  • Accuracy: 89.8% (4.3% better than en_core_web_lg)
  • Speed: 10x slower than lg model
