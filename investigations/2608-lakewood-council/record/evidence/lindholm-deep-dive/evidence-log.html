Identity re-check (2026-11-13)

QuestionFindingGrade
Is the councilmember the same person as the Oxford/chaplain academic?Documentary record says yes. Same domain (philiplindholm.com) with the 2013 academic bio and 2025 “CEO at Terry Wise & Associates” consulting page; same DVIDS 194th Wing chaplaincy; same early life (Decatur High School, Green River Running Start, Central Washington, Oxford); same wife/son names (Lori, Declan) in the DVIDS article and in public family profiles; same PO Box on the Puyallup Tribal Court list.Verified on documents; photo comparison inconclusive
Do the 2017/2019 photos match the 2025/2026 photos?Not cleanly. The earlier photos show a leaner man with darker hair and a longer, narrower face; the later photos show a fuller face and lighter hair. Could be aging/weight/lighting/retouching, or could be two different men. No biometric comparison performed.Unverified — do not publish a two-person theory without a facial/vital record
Is there a second Philip Lindholm with the same biography?None found. The 2013 site, DVIDS article, Wikipedia page, city bio, Chamber bio, campaign site, LinkedIn, and TEDx all describe one continuous career: academic/filmmaker → broker/consultant (The Lindholm Group; Terry Wise & Associates) → chaplain (CHI Franciscan; 194th Wing) → councilmember.No contrary record found
What would settle it?A birth certificate/marriage record; a second DVIDS or military record with a different DOB; a direct photo with both men; a family member’s public statement; or the PDC imaged F-1 with a DOB.Open

Caveat for copy: if the identity is used in publication, say “public records describe Lindholm as…” and avoid asserting a photo match. The documentary convergence is strong enough for reporting, but the photo discrepancy is a fair defense challenge and should be resolved before any story that relies on the Oxford/chaplain biography as the same person.
