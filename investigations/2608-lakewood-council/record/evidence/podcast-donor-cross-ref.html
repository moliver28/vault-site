Pattern Analysis

Timing Cluster

Three of the four newly discovered donor-guests share the same donation date: April 28, 2025 (O’Ban, Whalen, Herrera); Chris Van Vechten’s $100 came May 15, 2025. The April 28 tranche in the PDC file contains 15 contributions totaling $2,350, three of them from donor-guests. The same-day pattern is documented; coordination is inference, not evidence.
