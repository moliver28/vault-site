Philip Lindholm

Lakewood City Council, Position 3 (took office January 2026; term to 31 December 2029). Licensed Washington real estate broker, landlord, mediator, tribal-court advocate, podcaster, co-founder of a local news outlet. The case’s central subject: a part-time officeholder whose private interests run through the same policy area he votes on.

Identity, as the record carries it

  • Office — Position 3; the council is part-time and nonpartisan; the mayor is chosen by the council. [city page]
  • Licence — WA DOL 109485, Real Estate Broker, active. First issued 25 July 2011; current issue 26 June 2025; expires 25 July 2027; no disciplinary action. Licensee firm REAL-BROKER-LLC (21488, Bellevue) — not Terry-Wise & Associates, where HomeLight still lists him. [DOL, 2026-11-15]
  • The “16 years” claim — told the council on 4 May 2026 he had been “a licensed real estate professional for 16 years.” From 25 July 2011 that is 14 years 9 months, spanning sixteen calendar years. Publish as arithmetic, never as a dishonesty claim.
  • Credentials — the Oxford doctorate, chaplaincy and BBC/ITV work are self-reported or derivative; the merge with the Oxford scholar/chaplain is an inference, not a verified fact. See LW-Verification-Ledger.

Votes and conduct

  • 4 May 2026 — Ordinance-849 on rental-housing licensing. Carried on a voice vote; he is not recorded in opposition. He seconded the ADU amendment (carried 4–3) and voted against deleting self-certification (that motion failed 3–4). Said he had “personal experience with the rental safety program.” No disclosure of his rental ownership appears in the minutes; a transcript search found none in the video either.
  • 6 April 2026 — pulled Motion 2026-25 (the $71,181.85 abatement) from consent and abstained. Said: “I do not have a professional relationship with Mr. Wise, but I do have a long-standing friendship and former — he’s a former colleague of mine.” See Solberg and Terry-Wise.
  • No recusal on anything else in the 56 meetings reviewed. No PDC enforcement case exists against him (dataset through 2026-11-14).
