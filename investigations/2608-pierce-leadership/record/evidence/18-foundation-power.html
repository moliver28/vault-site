Foundation & Nonprofit Power in Pierce County

Pierce County Leadership Investigation (INV-2026-002)

Evidence File 18: Foundation Power Mapping

Compiled: August 17, 2026
Classification: Investigative Research — Not for Public Distribution

Executive Summary

This file maps the major philanthropic foundations operating in Pierce County, their leadership structures, grantmaking priorities, and interlocking board relationships with corporate and government power structures. Foundation and nonprofit boards serve as a significant but often invisible dimension of regional power — they channel millions in funding, shape policy agendas, and serve as a revolving door for corporate, government, and civic elites.

Key Overlap Themes

1. Labor-Civic Interlock (Nathe Lawver)
Nathe Lawver is the single most important interlock between organized labor and philanthropic power in Pierce County. He simultaneously:

  • Chairs United Way of Pierce County Board
  • Serves on GTCF Board
  • Serves as Executive Secretary, Pierce County Building & Construction Trades Council, AFL-CIO
  • Sits on Pierce County Workforce Development Board
    This grants labor a direct voice in both the largest community foundation and the largest fundraising organization in the county, while giving those institutions labor legitimacy.

2. GTCF-Government Axis
GTCF’s board has direct government representation that is unusual for a community foundation:

  • Rosie Ayala — current Pierce County Councilmember
  • Ryan Mello — former board member, now Pierce County Executive
  • Jacques Colon — City of Tacoma Strategic Manager (direct city government employee)
  • Ahlmahz Negash — Tacoma Power (municipal utility)
  • Timothy Allen — retired City of Tacoma Retirement Director
  • Sheila Edwards Lange — UW Tacoma Chancellor (public university)

This means GTCF effectively has direct government representation on its board while making grants that fund policy-adjacent work.

3. Dona Ponepinto’s Cross-Sector Influence
Ponepinto’s board seats span:

  • United Way of Pierce County (CEO)
  • Tacoma Art Museum (President)
  • Tacoma Community College (Board of Trustees Chair — state appointment)
  • Coordinated Care (managed care org)
  • Wellfound Behavioral Health Hospital
  • Workforce Development Council
  • Association of Junior Leagues International (President)
  • Tacoma-Pierce County Chamber of Commerce

She is one of the most networked nonprofit leaders in the county, with direct ties to state government (Inslee appointment), healthcare, and arts.

4. GTCF-TAM-UWPC Triangle
The GTCF board, Tacoma Art Museum board, and United Way board share multiple members:

  • Lori Forte Harnick: GTCF Chair Emeritus + TAM Secretary
  • Ali Modarres: Former GTCF Secretary + TAM Board
  • Jacqueline Justice: UWPC Board + TAM Board
  • Pamela Transue: GTCF Investment Committee + TAM Past President
  • Nathe Lawver: GTCF Board + UWPC Board Chair

5. Russell Family Foundation Links

  • Richard Woo (former TRFF CEO) serves on GTCF Board
  • Lyle Quasim (former Bates Tech president, former WA state cabinet) appears in TRFF history and serves on GTCF Board
  • Kathleen Simpson (TRFF CEO) is part of American Leadership Forum Tacoma/Pierce County

7. POLITICAL INFLUENCE CHANNELS

State-Level Appointments

  • Dona Ponepinto — Appointed by Gov. Jay Inslee to Tacoma Community College Board of Trustees (2019)
  • Erika Tucci (Cheney Foundation ED) — Chairs Gov. Jay Inslee’s Serve Washington Commission
  • Lyle Quasim — Former cabinet member for two Washington governors (prior to foundation roles)

Policy Advocacy Networks

  • United Way’s ALICE research and Guaranteed Income pilot are direct policy interventions
  • GTCF’s Community Reinvestment Project involves direct contracting with WA State Department of Commerce ($900K)
  • Jane’s Fellowship Program builds grassroots leadership pipeline in Pierce County — 144 graduates
  • United Way and GTCF both fund Foundation for Tacoma Students — a collective impact organization that interfaces with Tacoma Public Schools

Campaign Contributions & Political Ties

  • Ryan Mello moved directly from GTCF board to Pierce County Executive — the foundation board served as a networking and legitimacy platform
  • Dona Ponepinto serves on the Tacoma-Pierce County Chamber of Commerce board, a major political advocacy organization
  • GTCF board includes retired state legislator/judge Art Wang (former State Representative 27th LD)

8. CONCLUSION & INVESTIGATIVE LEADS

Key Findings

  1. Nonprofit/philanthropic power in Pierce County is heavily concentrated across a small network of interlocking boards, with GTCF, United Way, and Tacoma Art Museum forming a governance triangle.

  2. Government officials sit on grantmaking boards — Ryan Mello (now County Executive) and Rosie Ayala (Councilmember) have held GTCF board seats while holding elected office, creating potential conflicts of interest between funding decisions and government policy.

  3. Corporate influence flows through foundation boards — major banks (Umpqua, Bank of America, Columbia, Northwest), health insurers (Coordinated Care, Regence), and wealth management firms (Morgan Stanley, Mission Wealth) all have representation on foundation boards.

  4. Labor has a formal seat at the table through Nathe Lawver’s dual board roles, but labor representation is concentrated in building trades, not service/healthcare unions.

  5. The Russell Family Foundation is the primary environmental/climate funder in Pierce County, operating independently from the GTCF-United Way social-service nexus.

  6. Trust-based wealth ($207M GTCF, $77M TRFF, Cheney lumber fortune, Russell Investments fortune, PACCAR fortune) is being deployed through increasingly racial-equity and climate-focused grantmaking, while the board composition remains heavily weighted toward professional and corporate elites.

Suggested Follow-Up Investigations

  • Map campaign contributions from foundation board members to Pierce County elected officials
  • Analyze GTCF discretionary grant recipients for political/policy alignment with board member interests
  • Investigate Dona Ponepinto’s role at Coordinated Care and any intersection with United Way funding
  • Track Cheney Foundation grants against Erika Tucci’s state-level political connections (Serve Washington)
  • Profile Lyle Quasim’s full board network (GTCF + Forterra + state government history)
  • Examine the Foundation for Tacoma Students board and funding — key collective impact intermediary
