0. Why this person or group is on the map

  • Chyna Willman is the Board Chair of the Tacoma-Pierce County Chamber of Commerce for the 2025–2026 year. The Chamber is the main business-advocacy group in Pierce County. She speaks for the business community while the Chamber looks for a new CEO.
  • She runs the Chamber board at a time when it still holds seats on the Tacoma-Pierce County Economic Development Board (EDB), runs the Candidate Academy that trains people who want to run for office, and talks with the County Council and Tacoma City Council on business rules.

1. Who they are

  • The bottom line. Willman’s power is reputation and network, not money. As Chamber Board Chair, she runs the most important business-advocacy group in Pierce County. But the seat only lasts one year, and her own business (Grit City Wellness) is a small local fitness studio. Her real power is convening power: the Chamber runs the Candidate Academy that trains future local candidates, holds a seat on the Tacoma-Pierce County EDB, and lobbies the County Council and Tacoma City Council. The Chair role is a high-visibility, short-term leadership post — not a long-term economic lever.
  • Conflicts-of-interest flags: As a small-business owner, Willman’s stated Chamber priorities emphasize “business resilience.” That framing often pushes for fewer rules and more development. Defense: that is the constituency the Chamber exists to represent.
  • Analysis of alternatives: A defense-friendly reading: Willman is a typical rotating Chamber Chair with modest personal power. A critical reading: the Chamber shapes local candidate pipelines, the EDB, and council relations, so even a one-year Chair term gives her real agenda-setting power.
  • Confidence: High — Chamber’s own announcements, Willman’s LinkedIn, The News Tribune feature, and southsoundbiz.com coverage all corroborate the Chair role, the Grit City Wellness founding, and the Puyallup residence.
